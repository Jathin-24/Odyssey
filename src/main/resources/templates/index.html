Event Schedule & Rounds

Navigate through the 2-day entrepreneurial odyssey, from initial problem discovery to the investor grand finale.

📅 Day 1 (Oct 29) — Startup Formation & Strategic Challenge ⏱ 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM IST (AP Time) 📍 CV Raman Hall, KITS
⏰ 10:00 – 10:15 AM Round 1

Round 1: Launch

Welcome, event rules briefing, and team check-in.

⏰ 10:15 – 10:35 AM Round 2

Round 2: Problem Drop

Receive assigned problem statement and form initial hypothesis.

⏰ 10:35 – 11:35 AM Round 3

Round 3: Customer Hunt

Collect coordinator customer-review slips to gather real user insights.

⏰ 11:35 AM – 1:00 PM Round 4

Round 4: Solution Lab

Define target customer, value proposition, and initial MVP concept.

🍱 1:00 – 2:00 PM Break

🍱 Lunch Break

Refuel, network, and discuss team strategy.

⏰ 2:00 – 3:00 PM Round 5

Round 5: Strategic Condition Challenge

Receive a private surprise business condition and adapt strategy.

⏰ 3:00 – 5:00 PM Round 6

Round 6: Startup Development & Pitch Prep

Build prototype and prepare for presentation.

📅 Day 2 (Oct 30) — Crisis Response, Presentations & Investment ⏱ 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM IST (AP Time) 📍 CV Raman Hall, KITS
⏰ 10:00 – 11:30 AM Round 7

Round 7: Crisis Envelope / Market Shock

Handle a sudden market shock under tight time constraints.

⏰ 11:30 AM – 1:00 PM Preparation

Team Prep & Submission Verification

Finalize presentation slides and verify submission files.

🍱 1:00 – 2:00 PM Break

🍱 Lunch Break

Recharge before entering the pitching arena.

⏰ 2:00 – 3:30 PM Round 8

Round 8: Team Presentations

Present problem, solution, and journey to all participating teams.

⏰ 3:30 – 4:30 PM Round 9

Round 9: Investor Pitch

Pitch finalized venture and MVP directly to the investor panel.

🏆 4:30 – 5:00 PM Grand Finale

Awards & Closing Ceremony

Score consolidation, winner announcements, and closing remarks.
